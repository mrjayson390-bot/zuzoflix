About and rights

What Zuzoflix is

Zuzoflix is a place to watch translated and local films. Films are kept on our own storage and played to you through this site.

Where our films come from

  • Films owned by Zuzoflix, or that the owner has allowed us to show.
  • Films offered to us by filmmakers and distributors through the Submit page, after we check them.
  • Open films that carry a Creative Commons licence. Their credits are shown in the film description.
  • Films shown from the owner's own YouTube channel.

If you own a film here

If a film is shown without your permission, tell us on the report page. We hide the film quickly while we check.

If you make films

You can offer your film on the Submit your film page.
