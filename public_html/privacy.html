Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026. We keep what we collect to a minimum. Here is exactly what it is.

Watching films

You do not need an account to watch. We do not ask for your name or phone number to watch, and we do not sell your information. We count how many times each film is played, as a total, not linked to you.

Forms you send us

On the Submit your film and Report a problem pages we keep what you type (name, phone or email, and your message or film details) so that we can read it and reply. We also keep your internet address for a short time to stop spam. We use this only to deal with your message.

Cookies

Visitors get no tracking cookies and no advertising cookies. A sign-in cookie is used only for the site's own admin.

Other services we use

  • Cloudflare and our hosting company deliver the site and store films, and they handle your connection details to do that.
  • YouTube (in a privacy-friendly mode) plays trailers and films that come from YouTube. YouTube may collect information when you press play on those.
  • TMDB supplies film pictures, ratings and cast.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's text style.

Keeping and deleting

We keep messages only as long as we need them. To see or delete what you sent us, write to mrjayson390@gmail.com.

Children

The site is not aimed at young children. Parents should choose what their children watch.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and its date.
